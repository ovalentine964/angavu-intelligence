Mission

Give Every Worker a CFO

Every mama mboga deserves to know her profit. Every boda boda rider deserves to build credit. Every fundi deserves to track his business. Msaidizi makes this possible — free, voice-first, offline-first.

Free Forever

Msaidizi is free for every worker. No subscriptions, no premium tiers, no hidden costs. The tool is free. The intelligence it creates has value.

Voice First

Speak in your language — Swahili, Sheng, or any of 15+ African languages. Msaidizi understands. No typing needed.

Works Offline

No internet? No problem. Msaidizi runs entirely on your phone. AI models, voice recognition, business intelligence — all on-device.

Principles

What We Stand For

Workers Are the Product

Not the app. Not the AI. The worker's business data — their daily transactions, their growth, their creditworthiness — that's the product. We protect it like it's ours, because it's theirs.

Privacy Is Not a Feature

It's the foundation. Raw transactions never leave the device. GPS data never leaves the device. Contacts never leave the device. What leaves is anonymized, encrypted, and aggregated.

Build for the Margins

If it works for a mama mboga in Migori with no internet and a $50 phone, it works everywhere. Design for the hardest conditions first.

Compounding Intelligence

Every transaction makes the system smarter. Every worker makes every other worker's intelligence better. The flywheel compounds. The moat deepens.
